**Duration**: standard (2500 iterations, ~100 min)

## Analysis

### Resets
No visible resets throughout the clip. All robots remain upright across all 25 frames.

### Displacement
Robots show clear forward displacement relative to grid lines. Central robot displaces 2-3+ body lengths over the 12.5-second clip. Multiple robots tracking command arrows with visible position changes.

### Stability
All robots remain upright throughout. No falls, stumbles, or significant tilting visible in any frame.

### Gait
Clear cyclic leg movements visible between frames. Legs in different phases consistent with trotting gait. Good rhythmic pattern.

### Posture
Body stays relatively level. No visible dragging or elbow contact. Reasonable ground clearance.

### Leg Movement
Visible foot lift between frames. Front and rear legs show coordinated cycling. Some robots show slightly lower posture but still ambulatory.

### Population
5-6 of 7-8 visible robots show clear locomotion. High consistency across environments.

## Verdict
**LOCOMOTION** - Cyclic gait with visible forward displacement well exceeding 1 body length per episode.

## Summary
The compound sim-to-real changes (20Hz control, velocity-blind observation, servo velocity limit 4.29 rad/s, corrected body mass) produce clear locomotion. Policy learned to walk without velocity feedback. This establishes a valid new baseline for the sim-to-real config.

## Recommendation
This is a strong baseline. Consider:
1. Extended training (5000 iter) to see if reward can approach the old 487.9 level
2. Reward tuning for 20Hz control -- mode_time and velocity_threshold may need adjustment for fewer steps per gait cycle
3. The velocity tracking (4.99) is lower than old baseline (7.44) -- expected since velocity-blind removes direct feedback
