# EXP-816 Video Annotation

**Experiment**: EXP-816 - Extended training on sim2real config (5000 iter)
**Duration**: 5000 iterations (~200 min)
**Video**: rl-video-step-0-main.mp4 (single camera, 25 frames at 2fps)

## Analysis

### Resets
No visible resets. All robots remain upright throughout all 25 frames.

### Displacement
Clear forward displacement from frame 1 to frame 25. Central robot displaced 3+ body lengths over the clip. Multiple robots tracking command arrows with visible position changes.

### Stability
All robots upright throughout. No falls, stumbles, or tilting.

### Gait
Cyclic leg movements visible with clear leg extension changes between frames. Consistent with trotting gait.

### Posture
Body stays level. Good ground clearance. No dragging or elbow contact.

### Population
All 7-8 visible robots showing locomotion behavior. More uniform quality than EXP-815.

## Verdict
**LOCOMOTION** - Clear cyclic gait with forward displacement exceeding multiple body lengths.

## Summary
Extended training (5000 iter) produces slightly better reward (470.9 vs 459.5) with comparable video quality to EXP-815. The marginal improvement (+2.5%) suggests the standard 2500 iter is sufficient for this config. Extended training smooths penalties slightly (smoothness -1.47 vs -1.55, orientation -0.13 vs -0.16).

## Recommendation
Extended training provides marginal gains. The sim2real config is validated. Move to reward tuning for 20Hz control:
1. Adjust mode_time for 20Hz (0.25 may be too long at 20Hz -- only 5 control steps per mode)
2. Consider reducing mode_time to 0.20 or 0.15
3. Tune velocity_threshold for 20Hz gait cycle
