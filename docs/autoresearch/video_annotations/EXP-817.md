## Analysis

No resets. All robots upright throughout.

### Displacement
Clear forward displacement. Central robot displaced 2+ body lengths.

### Stability
All robots stable and upright. Good body posture. Orientation metric best ever (-0.064).

### Gait
Cyclic leg movements. Comparable quality to baseline (EXP-816).

### Population
All visible robots walking.

## Verdict
**LOCOMOTION** - Clear cyclic gait with forward displacement.

## Decision
**DISCARD** - Reward 439.8 vs baseline 470.9 (-6.6%). Lower air_time (2.63 vs 3.50) is a reward structure artifact from shorter mode_time threshold, not a behavior change. Orientation improved (-0.064 vs -0.13) but not enough to justify overall regression. mode_time=0.25 produces higher reward.
