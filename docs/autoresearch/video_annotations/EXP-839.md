# EXP-839 Video Review

**Experiment**: EXP-839 - Rough terrain standard (2500 iter)
**Task**: harold_rough (Harold-Velocity-Rough-v0)
**Camera**: main (single camera, wide/distant view)
**Frames**: 25 at 2fps (12.5s clip)

## Observations

- **Resets**: 0 visible. All robots remain upright throughout clip.
- **Displacement**: Clear forward displacement of all 3 visible robots over the clip. Multiple body lengths of travel.
- **Stability**: All robots upright across all frames. No falls or stumbles.
- **Gait**: Cyclic leg movements visible between frames.
- **Terrain**: Robots navigating across mixed terrain patches (flat, rough, slopes).

## Key Finding

Training metrics showed orientation degradation over time:
- At 60% progress: orientation=-0.097, reward=520.3 (peak)
- At 100% progress: orientation=-0.353, reward=497.3 (declining)

The best_agent.pt checkpoint (used for video) was saved near peak performance, which shows functional LOCOMOTION. Extended training on rough terrain causes the policy to become more aggressive and sacrifice body stability. This suggests rough terrain should use fast duration (625 iter) or early stopping rather than full 2500 iter.

## Verdict: LOCOMOTION

Robots walking on rough terrain with clear displacement. No resets. Policy learns to handle terrain but overfits with extended training.

## Recommendation

1. Rough terrain needs stronger orientation penalty to prevent posture degradation during long training
2. Or use fast duration (625 iter) which was optimal for this config on flat terrain too
3. For next experiment: increase base_orientation_weight in rough_env_cfg.py override to counteract terrain-induced tilt
