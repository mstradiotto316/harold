## Verdict: LOCOMOTION

## Decision: DISCARD

Stronger orientation weight does not solve the root cause (orientation degradation during extended training). Fast duration training is the proper solution.

## Recommendation

Revert to base_orientation_weight=-5. Use fast duration (625 iter) for rough terrain experiments. Try other tuning axes: foot_clearance, action_smoothness, or different terrain proportions.
