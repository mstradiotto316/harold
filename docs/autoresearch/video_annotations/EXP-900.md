# EXP-900 Video Analysis

## Experiment
foot_clearance_weight 3.0 -> 4.0 (higher foot lift for carpet walking)

## Verdict: LOCOMOTION

Forward walking with 10-12 body-length displacement, zero resets. But visible body tilt.

## Key Observations
- Foot clearance +33% but orientation 2x worse (-0.214 vs -0.11)
- Visible rear-down pitch in frames 2-8, some lateral roll oscillation
- Shuffling quality from -13% air time reduction
- Front/rear leg asymmetry — rear legs overworking

## Decision: DISCARD
Orientation regression too severe for hardware. Higher clearance destabilizes gait timing.
